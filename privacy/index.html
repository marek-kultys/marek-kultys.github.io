This is my personal site. I run analytics on it so I can see which work people actually read. This page explains what that means in practice, what it does not collect, and how to switch it off. Last updated in November 2026.

What is collected

I use Mixpanel to understand how the site is used. It records which pages you open, how far you scroll, what you click, and signals like clicking something that is not clickable. It also records a replay of your visit: a reconstruction of what the page looked like and where you moved and clicked on it.

Mixpanel stores this against an anonymous identifier kept in your browser. I do not know who you are, and I make no attempt to find out.

The data is sent to and held on Mixpanel's servers in the European Union, and session replays are kept for 30 days.

What is not collected

There is nothing on this site to fill in. No forms, no search box, no comments, no accounts, no newsletter. So there is nothing you can type here for anything to capture.

I do not run advertising, I do not use advertising or social media trackers, and I do not sell, share or trade any of this with anyone.

Storage on your device

Mixpanel keeps an identifier in your browser, under a name beginning mp_, and uses your browser's local database to buffer session replay data before sending it. Clearing your browsing data for this site removes both.

How to opt out

The site honours the Do Not Track setting in your browser. If you turn Do Not Track on, no analytics or session replay data is collected from your visit.

Any content blocker or privacy-focused browser that blocks cdn.mxpnl.com will also stop it, and nothing on the site depends on analytics to work.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub processes requests to the site, which involves your IP address, under their own privacy statement. I do not have access to server logs.

Getting in touch

If you want to know what the site holds about your visit, or want it deleted, write to me and I will sort it out. My email is on the contact page.
